Installation

Before you flash

This community release has been tested on Sealegs hardware and in 27 automated firmware suites. It comes as is. Install it at your own risk. Custom firmware may affect warranty coverage. Intellijel supports its official firmware.

Keep the original Sealegs.1.3.5.0.bin for recovery and note any settings you want to recreate. To return to stock, flash that file here or use the Intellijel Firmware Updater.

Step 1 · Local file

Build your firmware

  1. Download the original Sealegs.1.3.5.0.bin from Intellijel’s firmware directory.
  2. Select the original file below to build Redux directly. Save Sealegs.1.3.5.0+c.bin and use it in the USB step.

Step 2 · USB

Flash your Sealegs

Use desktop Chrome or Edge and a USB data cable. Power off the rack, connect the computer to the rear USB port, then hold the rear BOOT button as you power the rack on. Release BOOT and choose your saved Redux file below. After the write and readback verification, use Restart module, then power down, disconnect USB and power on normally.

How the patch and USB verification work

Your browser checks the SHA-256 of the official 1.3.5.0 file, applies a patch containing BRIBERY’s Seal Eggs features and the Redux changes to a copy in memory, then verifies the result against the 1.3.5.0+c release hash before offering the download. Your firmware stays on this computer.

If you already have a full Seal Eggs build with Tape Bright on or off, you can also select it here. The patcher recognizes either version and produces the same Redux file. In Redux you can switch Tape Bright on the module.

The USB step independently verifies the file you select. The updater checks the device’s flash layout, writes the image and reads it back for comparison. Keep power and USB connected until the updater reports completion. Restore stock before seeking official product support. Intellijel’s Sealegs boot procedure

Beyond stock

What changes on the panel

BRIBERY built Seal Eggs on Intellijel’s stereo delay. Redux gives the existing sounds independent switches and adds new ways to play Digi and Beads.

Open the options layer

Hold MOD SYNC alone for about one second, then release. The MOD light breathes white, alternating with teal in Beads or orange in Microloops. Tap buttons to change an option. Tap MOD SYNC again to save and exit. A green acknowledgment follows a successful changed-settings write.

To enter or leave an alternate engine, hold FREEZE/ALT + DELAY SYNC for about a second during normal play. Use BBD for Microloops and DIGI for Beads.

Launch a Pitch Throw

In Digi options, tap DELAY SYNC until magenta, then exit. Rest TIME/L briefly and move it about 2% or more. A shorter time pitches the echo up, a longer time pitches it down. The echo bends roughly five semitones and fades over two seconds above your regular delay.

Hold FREEZE/ALT in normal play to reposition TIME/L quietly. On release, the main delay waits until the slider crosses its old value. An active throw finishes its fade.

What was tested

An independent build reproduced Seal Eggs Redux 1.3.5.0+c byte for byte. All 27 automated firmware test suites passed, covering audio processing, button gestures, saved preferences and timing diagnostics. Listening tests on Sealegs hardware covered the model characters, Pitch Throw, Beads and all three Microloops ranges with a clock. Hardware testing covered a selection of patches and did not measure real-time deadlines.

For clocked Microloops, try a short trigger per quarter note. At 120 BPM, a simulated 50% square wave could register twice per beat. A 10 ms trigger registered once. The audition above uses that short-trigger timing.
